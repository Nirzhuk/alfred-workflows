# Deferred: Homebrew distribution

Homebrew is not part of Alfred's Polar paid-release path.

A public cask needs an anonymously downloadable official binary URL, which
would bypass Polar's purchaser/member authorization and personal signed
downloads. The public repository continues to provide source and build
instructions; official maintainer-built binaries are obtained from Polar.

Revisit Homebrew only if one of these is explicitly approved:

- an authenticated/private tap with a tested customer experience;
- a public official binary channel and revised commercial promise; or
- a source-only formula/cask that does not redistribute maintainer-signed paid artifacts.

Until then, do not publish or advertise a public Alfred cask.
